What changes going Material

Same screens and same content model as the iOS mockup — the platform grammar is what differs. Notes below track with the screen on the phone.

Journal — the start destination

  • Large top app bar collapses to the 64 dp small bar, and the surface shifts tone on scroll — M3 signals elevation with colour, not a shadow line.
  • Overflow ⋮ replaces the iOS avatar button; account lives behind it.
  • The today card is a filled tonal card in primary-container.
  • Recent notes go edge-to-edge with 72 dp rows — no inset card grouping.
  • Native adds: home-screen widget for the seal rail, ongoing notification while a seal confirms.

Plants

  • A docked search bar heads the list — search is a first-class M3 component, not a hidden field.
  • State reads as tonal chips (Sealed, Water due, Listed).
  • Swipe-to-dismiss on rows; pull-to-refresh on the list.

Plant detail

  • A back arrow, not a chevron-plus-label — and the system back gesture does the same thing, with predictive-back preview.
  • On-chain facts sit in an outlined card, tx hashes in monospace as on web.

Market

  • Simple / Advanced is an M3 segmented button — a check mark marks the selection, not a sliding thumb.
  • Invest confirms through BiometricPrompt.
  • Wallet connect uses Mobile Wallet Adapter — this is Android's native path, and it's better here than on iOS.
  • Play policy: keep token flows framed as utility/test, and expect the same scrutiny the web app already writes around.

Grow coach

  • Bubbles use asymmetric M3 corner shapes; the send button is a filled icon button.
  • The composer is a surface-container bar, not a translucent blur — M3 leans on tone over material blur.
  • Coach suggestions write into Today, which drives the daily notification.

Tokenise

  • Reached from the overflow menu and from a plant — not frequent enough for a nav destination.
  • Sealing runs as a foreground service with an ongoing notification until the devnet tx confirms.

Today

  • Drives the daily notification, a Glance/Compose home-screen widget, and the At a Glance slot.
  • Checkboxes use the brand as accent-color; ripple on the whole row.

Notifications

  • Called Notifications, not Inbox — it mirrors the system shade, and rows deep-link to their plant or offer.
  • Three channels users can mute independently: confirmations, adoptions, stage milestones.

Camera

  • CameraX preview + capture; the mode strip is a row of chips rather than an iOS mode wheel.
  • Frame guide keeps diagnosis photos comparable week to week.

Settings — Theme

  • Three values, one preference — the shape of AppCompatDelegate.MODE_NIGHT_*: no / yes / follow system.
  • A radio group, not iOS's checkmark list, and M3 switches whose thumb grows and gains a check when on.
  • Light isn't the dark scheme inverted: M3 container tones step down from the surface here, and the accent darkens to #6f5416 to hold contrast on paper while filled buttons keep the gold.
  • Use wallpaper colours is the Material You opt-in — off by default, since brand beats wallpaper for this product. Android-only; iOS has no equivalent.

Notes

Brand colours from app/styles/app.css are mapped onto M3 roles — brass #c9a55e becomes primary, forest #0f1a12 becomes surface, and container tones step up from it. That mapping is what a real ColorScheme in Compose would hold.

Material You dynamic colour is deliberately not adopted — the wax-seal catalog identity is the product, so brand colour wins over wallpaper-derived colour.

Metrics are dp at 412 × 915 (Pixel 8/9 class): 28 dp status bar, 64 dp small app bar, 80 dp navigation bar, 24 dp gesture inset.
