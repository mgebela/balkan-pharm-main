## 3. [post] — coach without replacing judgment

- Platform: Facebook
- Topic: coach

### Draft

An AI coach is useful when it reads what you already logged — last watering, last feed, which stage — and asks the next check.

It should not replace looking at the plant.

That’s the line we’re building to. The journal is the source of truth. The coach is a second pair of eyes on the notes, not a grower.

If you want to try that setup, the journal is free and needs no wallet: https://growto.live/
