1. Verdict

A $200–250k minimum on Futard, with growtoo as it is today, is a low-probability raise — roughly 8–12%. The board does not pay for a live product. It pays for in-tribe MetaDAO narrative plus a tiny floor that can oversubscribe.

Chance bands are judgment from the board, not a model. The 8–12% band is “same ask as GameByts / Mycorealms / Boltick, with less claimed revenue than GameByts and a harder legal story than Jurassic.” The 25–40% band assumes a 7-day window, MetaDAO-native campaign, and a Jurassic-style Labs token (ops only — not harvest rights).

4. growtoo vs the names that matter

Where growtoo loses

  • No public user, revenue, or retention number you are allowed to print. GameByts could. They still missed $250k.
  • Cannabis harvest rights are a worse legal object than a fossil in a Cayman SPV or a mushroom farm. Futard tokens are explicitly not equity, not a security, not a claim on an entity. Selling “adopt a plant / harvest” as the ICO is the wrong instrument.
  • Product is Devnet. $GROWTOO has no monetary value. On-chain proof is a journal trail, not a physical plant. Honest — and the Futard buyer is buying a token that can dump on day one (ICO float unlocks immediately).

Where growtoo is not vapor

  • Live journal at growto.live: auth, coach, camera, Stories, 14-day list gate, Devnet escrow program. Most refunded launches were a PDF and a vibe.
  • The Labs paper already uses the Jurassic shape: Raise I = operating company, Raise II = harvest SPV later. That is the only $200k pattern that actually cleared on this board.
  • Monthly $8k runway math is in the same band as Futard spending caps ($10–14k on GameByts / Manna). The mechanism will enforce the cap onchain if you finish.

8. Do not add investor yield to the ICO token

A coupon, APY, buyback, or “harvest share for token holders” is the wrong instrument. Futard’s own B1 text says the standard launch creates no dividend or revenue entitlement. The Labs paper already says this round is not a sale of harvest title and you will not copy yield onto a test token. Cannabis + a yield promise is also the Howey-shaped reading you cannot afford. The board already has Kimia for yield. You will not out-yield them. Mycorealms (“all revenue to the DAO”) still refunded.
