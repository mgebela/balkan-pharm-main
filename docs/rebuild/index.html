1. Product snapshot

growtoo is a free cannabis / CBD grow journal with reminders, a 3-day weather widget, in-app camera, an AI coach (Gemini), photos, growth-stage tracking, public Stories, and an optional Solana Devnet proof layer (Tokenise + Market). Two desks after signup:

Grower

Journal is the product. Wallet is optional. Tokenise (seal stages / seed NFT) and listing sit behind chainOptIn.

Adopter

Market / garden desk. Faucet and Invest are adopter-only. Journal plants are empty by default.

profileType is immutable after create (grower | adopter). Clients cannot flip it; Admin SDK can. Age gate is 18+. Product is a documentation tool, not legal advice. Follow the law where you grow.

6. Auth & identity

  • Firebase Auth: email/password + Google. Authorized domains include growto.live.
  • Signup writes users/{uid} with role: 'user' and profileType. Grower extra: growSetup (indoor / outdoor / mixed), homeCity. Adopter extra: adopterIntent, acceptedDevnet.
  • Live Gemini requires email_verified on the ID token. Unverified accounts get a local helper. After admin-verifying an email, the user must sign out/in.
  • solanaPubkey / walletVerified are written only by CF linkWallet (Admin SDK) after ed25519 verify. Clients may unlink (delete those fields).
  • chainOptIn is a user-profile flag for Tokenise/Market.
  • Admin UI is email-allowlisted, not role-only.

7. Data room

Primary user journal is one Firestore document: users/{uid}/app/state. Local-first: same arrays also live in localStorage (dnevnik-live-plants, -entries, -toolbox). Debounced sync ~500ms. Hard ceiling: Firestore 1 MiB per document. Photos used to be inline base64 and broke backup; they now go to Storage.

7.4 Storage

users/{uid}/journal/{kind}-{stamp}-{rand}.jpg. Rules: owner read/write. Max ~5 MB after compression. If Storage fails, UI may fall back to inline data URI and must then respect the 1 MiB state-doc guard.

7.5 Market eligibility (keep in sync)

Duplicated in app/js/list-eligibility.js and functions/list-eligibility.js.

  • Seed mint: not gated on coverage. Requires a real plantId in the grower’s state.
  • List / escrow activate: ≥ 14 elapsed days since first care createdAt, and care on ≥ 50% of min(elapsed, 180) distinct UTC days.
  • Backdated entry.date does not count. Dumping 90 logs in one session is one care day.
  • Listings with journalCoverageOk === false are hidden from the public tape.

12. What a rewrite must keep

  1. Stable Firebase UIDs and users/{uid} profiles. Do not force a second signup.
  2. Plant ids in the journal blob — they are foreign keys for seedMints.plantId, listings, and growth mints.
  3. Care semantics: eligibility is createdAt, not journal date. Changing that silently rewrites who can list.
  4. Split the state blob if you move to a framework: plants / entries / toolbox as subcollections, with a migration from the existing document. Watch the 1 MiB trap and photo URLs.
  5. Do not make profileType client-mutable without a product decision. Two desks are a trust boundary (faucet, invest, Tokenise).
  6. Wallet link stays server-verified. Never let the client set solanaPubkey.
  7. Public market board reads marketPublicTape, not a list of marketListings.
  8. Devnet honesty: no monetary value, no live harvest redemption, on-chain ≠ physical plant.
  9. i18n: keep EN source + HR/DE; keep internal stage/entry keys; add DE to Gemini if the UI stays trilingual.
  10. Admin allowlist stays email-based until you replace it deliberately.
  11. Chain workers and the Anchor program can stay while the UI is rewritten. The fragile part is the 9k-line app.js + localStorage dual-write, not the mint queue.
  12. Move WeatherAPI off the client on rewrite.

Suggested seams if you reshape rather than incinerate: keep Functions + chain queues + Firestore rules as the backend; replace app/ with a typed SPA; migrate state; keep locales/ key tree.
