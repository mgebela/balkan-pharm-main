# Futard.io vs growtoo — $200–250k raise

**Audience:** internal. Not a Futard application form and not a public pitch.  
**Date of board scrape:** 3 September 2026.  
**PDF:** [`docs/futard/growtoo-futard-raise.pdf`](futard/growtoo-futard-raise.pdf)  
**Original canvas:** Cursor canvas `futard-raise-vs-growtoo` (3 Sep 2026 chat).

Source: public [futard.io](https://www.futard.io/) homepage + launch pages that day. Platform stats then: **86 launches**, **$43.7M committed**, **2.21k funders**. Commitments are not cash kept — only launches that clear the **minimum** keep funds. Everything else refunds.

Not investment advice. Futard tokens are not equity. Cannabis remains illegal in many places; follow counsel. Do not publish user counts or mint counts that are not in `product-facts.md`.

## 1. Verdict

A **$200–250k minimum** on Futard, with growtoo as it is today, is a **low-probability raise — roughly 8–12%**. The board does not pay for a live product. It pays for in-tribe MetaDAO narrative plus a tiny floor that can oversubscribe.

| Band | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **~8–12%** | $200–250k as the **minimum**, today |
| **~25–40%** | **$60–80k min**, take up to $200k if the book oversubscribes |
| **8 / 86** | Launches that cleared min (that day) |
| **1** | $200k+ ask that cleared (Jurassic Finance) |

Chance bands are judgment from the board, not a model. The 8–12% band is “same ask as GameByts / Mycorealms / Boltick, with less claimed revenue than GameByts and a harder legal story than Jurassic.” The 25–40% band assumes a 7-day window, MetaDAO-native campaign, and a Jurassic-style Labs token (ops only — not harvest rights).

**That URL is not a growtoo launch.** [futard.io/launch/Ffady…](https://www.futard.io/launch/FfadyEpRPJEbJHDvQ9ZeCBzkBjX1GZtEVNgMFogsfdq8) is **GameByts** — a live skins marketplace that asked $250k, got $57,916 (23%) from 13 wallets, and is refunding. Two wallets put in $50k and it still missed. Treat it as the closest “real product + $250k ask” analog, not as growtoo’s page.

## 4. growtoo vs the names that matter

### Where growtoo loses

- No public user, revenue, or retention number you are allowed to print. GameByts could. They still missed $250k.
- Cannabis harvest rights are a worse legal object than a fossil in a Cayman SPV or a mushroom farm. Futard tokens are explicitly not equity, not a security, not a claim on an entity. Selling “adopt a plant / harvest” as the ICO is the wrong instrument.
- Product is Devnet. $GROWTOO has no monetary value. On-chain proof is a journal trail, not a physical plant. Honest — and the Futard buyer is buying a token that can dump on day one (ICO float unlocks immediately).

### Where growtoo is not vapor

- Live journal at growto.live: auth, coach, camera, Stories, 14-day list gate, Devnet escrow program. Most refunded launches were a PDF and a vibe.
- The Labs paper already uses the Jurassic shape: Raise I = operating company, Raise II = harvest SPV later. That is the only $200k pattern that actually cleared on this board.
- Monthly $8k runway math is in the same band as Futard spending caps ($10–14k on GameByts / Manna). The mechanism will enforce the cap onchain if you finish.

## 5. The model that can actually take $200–250k

Do not set the **minimum** at $200–250k. Futard is all-or-nothing on the floor. Set the floor where the board sometimes clears, and take $200–250k only if the book oversubscribes (same as Jurassic: $200k kept from a $15M book).

| Piece | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Floor** | **$60–80k minimum.** Completes cluster at $10–60k plus two in-tribe exceptions (ordr $150k, Jurassic $200k). $80k is aggressive but not GameByts-suicidal. |
| **Take** | **$200k accept / $250k hard max.** If the book fills, accept $200k (Jurassic FDV template: 25.8M supply, 10M ICO, ~$516k FDV at $0.02). Stretch to $250k only if you already have named wallets in the book. |
| **Window** | **7 days, not 1 hour.** Jurassic ran 8–15 May 2026. You need time for MetaDAO / Ownership FM / Futard cult to notice. A 1-hour window is for people who already have the chat. |

### Copy Jurassic — do not copy GameByts / Mycorealms

| Piece | Copy Jurassic | Do not copy GameByts / Mycorealms |

## 7. If Mainnet ships before Futard

The Labs paper funds Mainnet **from the raise**. Doing it first means you pay for deploy, audit, SOL, and counsel out of pocket. It helps the story. It does **not** flip the $200k floor. Boltick and GameByts were already live and still refunded.

A scoped pilot is the only version that is honest: a few listings, USDC settlement, no harvest, no faucet, no $GROWTOO coupon.

| What | Devnet today | Mainnet before Futard |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| Journal / coach / Stories | Unchanged. Firebase. No wallet needed. | Unchanged. Mainnet does not make the journal truer. |
| 14-day / 50% list gate | Process evidence. Fake logs still possible. | Same gate, now with real money. Fabricated trails become a real-loss bug. Attestation must harden or you do not list. |
| $GROWTOO | No monetary value. Faucet OK. | If it has a price, the faucet dies and it is a real token. Do not make it the investor yield. Prefer USDC on the board so $GROWTOO stays a points/fee chip or stays off. |
| Plant NFT + adopt-stake | Test. Half-lock is a mechanic, not a return. | Real USDC in escrow. Still “follow one plant,” still not a Labs dividend. Harvest claim stays off until Raise II. |
| Labs / Futard token | Does not exist. | Still no coupon. Story gets better (“desk already settles”) but the token is still treasury + cap + later origination. |
| Harvest SPV | Mocked. | Still mocked. Mainnet does not legalize a crop. Do not turn redemption on. |
| Legal / ops | Test assets, operator mint queue. | Need audit, real key ceremony, kill-switch, ToS for real money, counsel memo before first listing. Cannabis + real USDC is the line the Labs paper said to cross after the raise. |
| $200k Futard odds | ~8–12% as a minimum. | Maybe mid-teens if there is public Mainnet volume and clickable growers. Floor should still be $60–80k. Mainnet is not Jurassic. |

## 8. Do not add investor yield to the ICO token

A coupon, APY, buyback, or “harvest share for token holders” is the wrong instrument. Futard’s own B1 text says the standard launch creates **no dividend or revenue entitlement**. The Labs paper already says this round is not a sale of harvest title and you will not copy yield onto a test token. Cannabis + a yield promise is also the Howey-shaped reading you cannot afford. The board already has Kimia for yield. You will not out-yield them. Mycorealms (“all revenue to the DAO”) still refunded.

| Layer | What it is | Who gets cash / rights | When live |
|-------|------------|------------------------|-----------|
| **A · $GROWTOO** | Devnet test currency. Stage rewards, faucet, board asks. | Nobody. No monetary value. Do not migrate this into an investor coupon. | Now (test only) |
| **B · Plant NFT + adopt-stake** | One plant, 14-day trail. Sale or 50/50 care lock. | Grower gets the sale / unlocked half. Adopter follows one plant. Not a Labs dividend. | Devnet now. Mainnet only after counsel. |
| **C · Labs token (only if you do Futard)** | Treasury + $8k/mo cap + futarchy. Same job as $RAWR: operate the desk. | No coupon. Designed later: origination fee on each Raise II SPV (Jurassic used 10% USDC + 5% of that SPV’s supply — numbers for counsel, not a live promise). | If the Futard min clears. Fee is off until Raise II exists. |
| **D · Harvest SPV token** | One batch, one vehicle, own token, operating agreement. | SPV holders — that is the only place “investors” buy crop economics. Labs takes a disclosed origination cut, not the harvest. | Raise II, after entity + memo. Not this raise. |

Designed Mainnet market split (not live, not a Futard slide): grower keeps the majority of a listing; Labs takes a thin escrow / listing fee; adopt-stake stays a care lock, not an APY. Exact percents wait for counsel. Do not print them as a return.

### If you insist on a $200k minimum

You need a Jurassic-class week: in-tribe founder, a one-line meme (“journal first, token second”), a Cayman Labs story, and a book that is already loud before the form goes live. Without that, the honest base case is GameByts — $50–80k pledged, refund, you look weak for the next try (Mycorealms had to come back three times and still missed).
