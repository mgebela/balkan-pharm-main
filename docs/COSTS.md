# growtoo use of funds — cost catalog

**Audience:** internal. Where Raise I cash should go, what a month of $8k actually buys, and a full menu of possible costs.  
**Date:** 4 September 2026.  
**PDF:** [`docs/costs/growtoo-use-of-funds.pdf`](costs/growtoo-use-of-funds.pdf)

This is **not** a priced round, not a Futard form, and not a founder employment contract. Figures are planning numbers in **USD**. Legal and harvest SPV costs need counsel before anyone spends them.

Related: Labs paper (`docs/growtoo-labs-200k-proposal.pdf`, `/pitch/labs/`) · Futard scan (`docs/FUTARD.md`) · product spec (`docs/PRODUCT.md`).

---

## 1. Three envelopes

Futard is all-or-nothing on the **floor**. Plan spend for the floor you set. Take $200–250k only if the book oversubscribes.

| Envelope | Cash in | Role | Months of $8k runway |
|----------|---------|------|----------------------|
| **A · Floor** | **$80,000** | Honest Futard minimum (see Futard scan). Survive 12 months thinner. | 12 × **$5,000** (or 10 × $8k and a shorter year) |
| **B · Fill** | **$200,000** | Canonical Labs paper. Same shape as Jurassic Raise I. | 12 × **$8,000** = $96k, plus legal + Mainnet buckets |
| **C · Stretch** | **$250,000** | Only if named wallets are already in the book. Extra $50k is not a second salary. | Same $8k/mo; extra goes to counsel, audit, domain, design |

Do not invent a fourth envelope that pays a $14k/mo team on an $80k floor. GameByts asked **$14,000/mo** on a **$250k** raise and still refunded.

---

## 2. Recommended split if $200k fills (envelope B)

Same 50 / 15 / 35 as the Labs paper. Legal and Mainnet are **separate buckets**. They are not paid from the monthly cap.

| Share | USD | Bucket | What it is for |
|-------|-----|--------|----------------|
| **50%** | **$100,000** | Runway | 12 months operations. Monthly allowance **$8,000**. Product, founder draw, contractors, hosting, coach, chain fees, small marketing. |
| **15%** | **$30,000** | Legal | Entity, IP assignment into Labs, cannabis/RWA memo, future SPV terms, Futard B1 / Cayman SPC if you launch there. Heavier than a collectible because cultivation rules differ by jurisdiction. |
| **35%** | **$70,000** | Mainnet + cohort | Scoped Mainnet pilot (audit, deploy, SOL, attestations), 30–50 real growers, 30-day retention work, Raise II term sheet — **not** buying a harvest. |

**Rule:** spend above $8k in a month from runway, or any legal/Mainnet draw outside a written decision log, needs approval (raise lead now; futarchy later if a DAO wrapper exists).

**$8k × 12 = $96,000.** The extra $4,000 in the $100k runway line is a buffer (FX, one overage month, tax reserve). Do not treat it as a 13th salary.

## 3. What $8,000 / month is (and is not)

On Futard this number is the **on-chain spending cap**: team may draw this much from the treasury without a new proposal. It is **not** “founder salary = $8k” plus a team on top.

| Launch | Monthly cap | Notes |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **growtoo (designed)** | **$8,000** | Conservative vs the board. Single operator + thin contractors. |
| GameByts | **$14,000** | Marketing, team, infra, anti-fraud, marketplace. Still missed a $250k floor. |
| Solar (same board) | **$14,000** | Same $14k template many launches copy. |
| Manna / similar | **$10–14k** | Same band cited in the Futard scan. |

Copying $14k because “others ask it” is how you empty a $80–100k floor in seven months. Stay at **$8k** unless the book is Jurassic-class and you have a second named operator.

### Inside the $8k — recommended month (envelope B)

Founder is also the engineer. Draw is pay for shipping, not a silent extra headcount.

| Line | / month | / year | Notes |
|------|---------|--------|--------|
| **Founder draw** | **$4,000** | $48,000 | Product + engineering + ops. CEE/HR-realistic; below a US founder salary on purpose so the cap still funds the desk. |
| Contract design / UI | $800 | $9,600 | Averaged. Some months $0, a landing/Futard page month $2–3k. |
| Marketing / community | $1,200 | $14,400 | Not paid ads as the thesis. AMA, English Futard page, one share-object plant, MetaDAO time. Community agent is already built. |
| Infra (see §5) | $400 | $4,800 | Firebase, Gemini, RPC, Netlify, weather, mail. |
| Buffer / tools / contractors | $1,600 | $19,200 | Extra eng week, accountant, translations, unexpected SOL. |
| **Total** | **$8,000** | **$96,000** | Must fit the cap. |

If you hire a second person, their pay **replaces** buffer + part of founder draw or design — it does not sit on top of $8k.

### Founder draw — what “like others” actually means

| Style | Monthly to founder | Honest read |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Recommended** | **$4,000** inside $8k cap | Operator can live; desk still has $4k for work. |
| Lean floor (envelope A) | **$2,500–3,000** | 12 months on $80k only works if founder draw drops. |
| “US founder” | $8,000+ | Then the cap **is** salary and there is no product budget. Do not do this. |
| GameByts-shaped | $14,000 team | Wrong floor. Do not copy. |

Founder draw is contractor compensation for work, not a dividend on a Futard token. Futard B1 does not create a salary entitlement; the cap is a **maximum**, not a paycheck.

## 7. Where money should **not** go

- Native store apps.
- A second visual identity.
- Paid user acquisition as the Futard thesis.
- LP / yield / buyback as a use-of-funds slide (Futard already has the machine; B1 forbids a dividend reading).
- Harvest inventory, shipping, warehouse.
- Inflating the monthly cap to $14k “because GameByts did.”
- Founder draw **plus** a full-time engineer **plus** $8k cap.
- Publishing mint counts or waitlists that are not in `product-facts.md`.
