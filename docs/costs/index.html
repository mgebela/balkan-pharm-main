1. Three envelopes

Futard is all-or-nothing on the floor. Plan spend for the floor you set. Take $200–250k only if the book oversubscribes.

3. What $8,000 / month is (and is not)

On Futard this number is the on-chain spending cap: team may draw this much from the treasury without a new proposal. It is not “founder salary = $8k” plus a team on top.

growtoo (designed)$8,000Conservative vs the board. Single operator + thin contractors.

Copying $14k because “others ask it” is how you empty a $80–100k floor in seven months. Stay at $8k unless the book is Jurassic-class and you have a second named operator.

7. Where money should not go

  • Native store apps.
  • A second visual identity.
  • Paid user acquisition as the Futard thesis.
  • LP / yield / buyback as a use-of-funds slide (Futard already has the machine; B1 forbids a dividend reading).
  • Harvest inventory, shipping, warehouse.
  • Inflating the monthly cap to $14k “because GameByts did.”
  • Founder draw plus a full-time engineer plus $8k cap.
  • Publishing mint counts or waitlists that are not in product-facts.md.
