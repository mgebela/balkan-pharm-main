02 · The gap

Beautiful token. Weak evidence underneath.

Grow data is manual, inconsistent, and difficult for an outside party to verify.

Evidence is an afterthought

Often added after the token already exists

One-time attestation

A one-time attestation goes stale

A number, not a history

Holders get a number, not a living history

The opportunity is to build the evidence layer before the asset layer.

03 · Order of operations

Journal. Verify. Seal. Then adopt.

The free journal is the top of funnel. The asset layer is optional.

Journal

Logs, photos, illness checks, stages, and grower activity.

Verify

Evidence is organised around the plant’s documented history.

Seal

A stage-linked proof asset can be minted on Solana Devnet.

Adopt

A backer follows a specific plant and its evidence trail.

09 · The raise

The raise $150,000–$200,000 Pre-seed

The capital will fund three de-risking milestones. The round is designed to turn an early ecosystem project into a validated, fundable product with measurable user evidence.

  • 1 · Legal — Establish the legal entity and regulatory framework for the RWA model.
  • 2 · Mainnet — Move the product from Solana Devnet to a properly scoped Mainnet pilot.
  • 3 · Growers — Expand from early testers to approximately 30–50 growers and demonstrate 30-day retention.
