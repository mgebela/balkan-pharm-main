# Stage-by-stage environment and care targets (cannabis / hemp)

Stage keys used in the app: klijanje=Germination, sadnica=Seedling, vegetativna=Vegetative,
cvjetanje=Flowering, susenje=Drying/harvest prep.

General principle: young plants want higher humidity and lower light intensity; mature
flowering plants want lower humidity (mold risk) and can handle more light and feed. Move
gradually between stages rather than making big jumps.

## Germination (klijanje)
- Temp: 22–25°C (72–77°F). Humidity: 70–90% if using a dome/propagator to keep the seed
  moist; less critical once the seed is in soil and covered.
- Watering: keep medium moist, never soaked. Seeds/seedlings rot easily in waterlogged soil.
- Light: not critical yet for a seed in the dark, but once it sprouts, gentle light (avoid
  intense direct light on a fresh sprout).
- Feeding: none. Seeds and fresh sprouts don't need nutrients — the seed itself has enough
  reserves for the first 1–2 weeks.
- Typical duration: 3–10 days from seed to visible sprout.

## Seedling (sadnica)
- Temp: 22–26°C (72–79°F). Humidity: 60–70%, tapering down as roots establish.
- Watering: light and frequent, small amounts — the root system is still tiny, so a full
  adult watering will drown it. Let the top layer dry between waterings.
- Light: gentle intensity, 18/6 (18h light / 6h dark) if using photoperiod lighting; slightly
  further from the light source than a vegetative plant would tolerate.
- Feeding: none or very light (quarter-strength) — seedlings are sensitive to nutrient burn.
- Typical duration: 2–3 weeks until 3–4 sets of true leaves.

## Vegetative (vegetativna)
- Temp: 22–28°C (72–82°F) day, a few degrees cooler at night is fine. Humidity: 40–60%.
- Watering: water when the top 2–3cm of soil is dry; water until slight runoff to flush salts.
- Light: 18/6 for photoperiod strains (or 24/0, grower preference); autoflowers don't need a
  light-schedule change to flower — they flower on their own timeline regardless of light hours.
- Feeding: full-strength balanced (N-leaning) nutrients; this is the main growth-building
  phase, so consistent feeding and training (topping/LST) happens here.
- Watch for: stretch just before flip to flower — plants can double in height in the first
  2 weeks of flower, so leave headroom.

## Flowering (cvjetanje)
- Temp: 20–26°C (68–79°F) — keep it slightly cooler than veg, especially at night. Humidity:
  40–50% early flower, dropping to 30–40% by late flower to reduce bud rot risk.
- Watering: adjust as canopy and root mass grow — larger plants drink more; still let the
  medium dry appropriately between waterings, overwatering risk increases with bud weight.
- Light: 12/12 for photoperiod strains (this triggers flowering); autoflowers keep the same
  schedule as veg since they flower by age, not light change.
- Feeding: shift to a bloom-leaning (P/K) nutrient line; taper nitrogen in the final 2–3 weeks.
- Watch for: humidity + dense colas = highest botrytis/bud rot risk window, inspect regularly.

## Late flower / flush (last 1–2 weeks before harvest)
- Many growers "flush" with plain pH-balanced water only for the final 1–2 weeks to use up
  stored nutrients before harvest — this is a common but debated practice, not a strict
  requirement; some grow mediums (coco, hydro) benefit from it more than living soil.
- Watch trichomes (cloudy vs. amber under magnification) rather than a fixed calendar date to
  judge harvest timing — calendar estimates from breeders are a starting guide, not a rule.

## Drying / harvest prep (susenje)
- Temp: 15–21°C (60–70°F). Humidity: 50–60% RH, with gentle airflow (not directly on the
  buds — indirect airflow in the room).
- No light needed during drying — dry in the dark to protect cannabinoids/terpenes.
- Typical duration: 7–14 days until small stems snap rather than bend. Slower, cooler drying
  generally preserves more aroma than fast, hot drying.
- After drying: curing in sealed jars (opened briefly daily — "burping" — for the first 1–2
  weeks) improves smoothness and aroma over the following weeks.

## Quick VPD note
If tracking environment via Tools, remember temperature and humidity interact — the same
humidity reading is riskier at a higher temperature. As a simple rule of thumb: if buds look
dense and humidity readings are creeping toward the top of a stage's range, prioritize airflow
before anything else.
