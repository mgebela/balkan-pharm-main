01 · The Problem

Cultivation runs on guesswork, paper, and fragmented tools.

CBD growers — from passionate hobbyists to multi-site producers — still rely on memory, scattered notes, and generic forum advice to make decisions that directly impact yield, quality, and compliance.

Manual & inconsistent logging

Notebooks, spreadsheets and phone photos scattered across cycles. No structure, no continuity, no learning between grows.

No insight, only data

Even when growers record everything, they can’t connect cause and effect. Patterns hide in plain sight; mistakes repeat each cycle.

Costly, late interventions

Stress, deficiencies, and environmental drift are spotted only when visible damage appears — often days too late to fully recover.

“A bad cycle costs weeks of work and thousands of euros. Most of that loss is preventable — if someone or something had warned you in time.”

02 · The Solution

A digital grow journal that thinks with you.

Dnevnik.live is more than logging. Every entry feeds an AI engine that learns your strains, your setup, and your habits — then proactively guides decisions from germination to harvest.

02

03

Predictive, contextual alerts

Smart notifications fire before stress is visible — feeding drift, VPD anomalies, growth-stage interventions, all in one inbox.

04

Knowledge that compounds

A hybrid of structured agronomic protocols and adaptive learning — the system gets smarter every cycle, for every user.
