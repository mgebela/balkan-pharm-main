How to adopt a plant

  1. 1
    Browse the market

    Open offers are sealed plant tokens with a journal trail behind them.

  2. 2
    Connect a test-network wallet

    Phantom or Solflare on Devnet — tap the wallet pill in the header when you are ready to invest.

  3. 3
    Back with $GROWTOO

    Instant sale moves the plant token to you now. Adopt stake: half now, half unlocks as the grower keeps logging care.

  4. 4
    Follow progress here

    Adopted plants land in this garden. Watch stages and monthly unlock status.

Test network only — $GROWTOO has no monetary value. You’re learning the trust flow before real stakes exist.
