02 · The gap

RWA starts at the token. The evidence is an afterthought.

Tokenising a physical thing is easy. Proving somebody actually tended it — every week, for months, in a way a stranger can re-derive — is the part nobody wants to build. So it gets replaced with a PDF and a signature.

04 · The journal

The half that works without a wallet.

A grower can use growtoo for a full season and never touch Solana. That is deliberate — it is how the evidence exists before anyone has a reason to fake it.

Stages & care log

Watering, feeding, transitions — each entry timestamped and plant-linked

Month view

Calendar over real logs, with coach due dates on their days

Photos

In-app camera or attach — compressed client-side, stored in Firebase Storage

Measurements

pH, EC, runoff, temperature — numbers the coach can actually reason over

Stories

Opt-in public write-ups on journal.growto.live

Offline-first

Local write, cloud sync after — the log survives a bad signal

05 · Grower side

A coach that has read your journal.

The coach is multimodal and grounded — it gets the plant's stage timing, recent weather, logged measurements and a photo, not just a question. It is a reason to log properly, which is the point.

Live coach

gemini-2.0-flash over a curated knowledge base — deficiencies, IPM, pH and substrate, light and canopy, chemotypes and legal limits, harvest and cure, stage targets, outdoor climate by latitude.

Plant camera

Full in-app capture. One shot goes two ways: Ask coach for a diagnosis, or Log to journal as dated evidence on that plant.

Skill-aware

The coach reads how the grower writes and teaches at their level — a first-season closet grower and a 5 ha field operation get different answers.

Honest failure

Unverified email, quota, network — each says which one. It falls back to a local helper rather than pretending to have looked at the photo.

06 · Gamification

The quests are the mint gate.

This is the part that makes the whole thing work. The game layer is not points bolted onto a product — it is the evidence collector. To seal a stage you must complete real, logged care, and each completed quest carries the ids of the journal entries that prove it.

1 Link a journal plant — the token points at a real plant, not a name
2 Log the growth stage — transition recorded in the journal
3 Log watering — for this stage, since the last one
4 Log feeding — required from seedling onward

No shortcut, no “skip”, no paying past it. Miss a quest and the seal button does not fire — and the proof ids mean the requirement can be audited after the fact.

08 · Adopter side

Half now. Half only if the care happens.

An adopter stakes on one specific plant and watches it. The grower is paid immediately for posting — and paid the rest only by keeping the log, every month, until harvest.

1 Adopter pays the full price into a care escrow, not to the grower
2 50% releases to the grower on settle; the NFT goes to the adopter
3 50% stays locked until harvest is claimed
4 A month qualifies at ≥12 distinct days of plant-linked care
5 Harvest is all-or-nothing across every month from adopt to claim

Live counters

The adopter sees the month timeline and day count, not a promise

Stage sync

Card stage follows the grower's real journal entries

Failure refunds

If the months don't qualify, the locked half returns to the adopter

10 · Why Solana

A care event is small, frequent and worthless alone.

This design writes often and settles in small amounts. That rules out most chains on fees alone before anything else is considered.

What the chain is for

  • Atomic swap of NFT and payment — no partial settle
  • An escrow the platform cannot quietly drain
  • A stake that unlocks on a rule, not on our say-so
  • Transfer costs that survive months of small events

What it is not for

  • Storing the journal — that stays off-chain and private
  • Proving a photo is real — it proves a photo is dated
  • Replacing lab testing or legal compliance
  • Speculation — $GROWTOO is a devnet test asset
