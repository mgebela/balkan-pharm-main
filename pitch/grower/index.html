05 · Optional

Proof, only if you want it.

Most growers never touch this part, and the journal works fine without it. But if you want a record other people can check — a buyer, a partner, or just your future self — each completed stage can be sealed on Solana with a transaction anyone can open.

01

Log the stage

Normal journal work. Nothing extra to learn.

02

Seal it

Optional mint tied to that journal entry. Wallet needed only here.

03

Share the link

Anyone can open the transaction and check the trail themselves.

Sealed on-chain when you choose, never because you must.

Today this runs on Solana’s public test network (Devnet). The transactions are real; the tokens are test assets with no monetary value.
