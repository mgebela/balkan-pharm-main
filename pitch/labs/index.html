What we’re building

growtoo Labs originates plant history, seals completed stages on Solana, and (later) structures harvest SPVs. Authentication today is the journal trail plus an operator check. Custody of a living plant stays with the grower. Ownership of a future harvest share would live in the SPV — only after counsel signs off.

  1. Log it — live now Free grow journal: watering, feeding, stages, photos, illness check, AI coach, weather, stories. No wallet. Indoor, outdoor, or mixed.
  2. Seal it — Solana Devnet Optional stage-linked plant tokens, $GROWTOO test rewards, on-chain escrow, instant sale or adopt-stake (half now, half as care continues). Listing needs 14 days of care, at least half those days logged. Same-day mint-and-list is blocked.
  3. Hold it — Raise II, after legal Each documented harvest batch as a dedicated SPV with its own token. Holders receive economic rights via the operating agreement. Physical redemption is specified, mocked on Devnet, not a live pipeline.

Why this round is not a token sale

Jurassic could put Labs on a permissionless ICO because the first asset is a museum-grade fossil with off-chain custody. A cannabis-adjacent harvest right cannot be sold that way until an entity and a regulatory path exist. $GROWTOO on Devnet has no monetary value by design. This $200k is cash into Labs so that Raise II is even possible.

Nothing in this paper is an offer of securities, a promise of yield, or a claim on physical plant material. Designed economics below are a model, not a live instrument.

Why plants

  • Evidence is the product. Most RWA desks tokenise a promise, then scramble for paperwork. growtoo inverts that: the journal is the funnel; the token is optional.
  • Living, not static. A fossil is authenticated once. A plant accrues a trail every watering. Adopt-stake already pays the grower to keep logging.
  • Recurring origination. Cycles repeat. Labs does not need a nine-figure specimen every year to have a next SPV.
  • Rules are opening, tooling is not. Growers still reconstruct week nine from a notebook and a camera roll. Documentation is becoming expected; the desk for it is missing.
  • Universally legible. “Follow this plant” does not require a DeFi primer. Crypto stays behind a Profile unlock.

Upside case

We are not selling a 100× venture outcome. We are selling a real operating company with a free top-of-funnel (the journal) and a later origination business (harvest SPVs).

A single compliant harvest SPV, once Raise II is legal, generates origination and platform fees for Labs. Growers keep the majority share under the designed model; adopters enter at a stage-weighted stake; Labs takes the market/escrow layer. Five to ten documented batches over two seasons is a realistic pipeline only if growers actually journal — which is why 30-day retention is a Raise I milestone, not a slide-deck vanity metric.

The lending market against SPV tokens that Jurassic scheduled for Q4 is out of scope for growtoo until harvest rights are real. We will not copy yield onto a test token.

Operating rules

Monthly allowance

$8,000 / month from the runway bucket without a new approval. Larger draws from legal or Mainnet buckets still require a written decision log (date, amount, purpose).

Harvest lockup

Labs will not bring a proposal to sell physical harvest rights until Raise II exists and counsel has signed the SPV. No “specimen flip” of a living crop on the back of this $200k.

Emergency

If a listing or mint is at legal or safety risk (seizure, platform abuse, fabricated trail), the operator may freeze or delist first and ratify after. Narrow clause: asset and user protection, not a change to the raise purpose.

Brand and IP

The growtoo name, mark, and product remain with the founder until assigned into the Labs entity formed with this capital. Custom product code developed for Labs is intended to sit in that entity. Open-source libraries, Firebase, and Solana programs stay under their own licenses. Domain: growto.live.
