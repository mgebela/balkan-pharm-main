02 · The gap

Beautiful token. Weak evidence underneath.

Grow data is manual, inconsistent, and difficult for an outside party to verify.

Evidence is an afterthought

Often added after the token already exists

One-time attestation

A one-time attestation goes stale

A number, not a history

Holders get a number, not a living history

The opportunity is to build the evidence layer before the asset layer.

06 · Economics

Evidence-backed harvest assets.

The weights are a designed Devnet model, not a live financial instrument. Nothing in this deck is an offer, a guarantee, or a promise of yield or returns. The legal and regulatory structure will be established before any Mainnet financial model.

Grower

Documents the plant’s history and keeps the majority share under the designed model.

Adopter

Supports a specific plant, with weighting based on stage of entry.

Platform

Provides journaling, verification, escrow, settlement, and the market layer.

07 · Why now

Documentation is becoming expected. The tooling is still missing.

The immediate goal is validation: prove that growers will keep useful records and that the evidence makes a plant more trustworthy to an adopter.

GrowersNeed a simple way to maintain a reliable plant history
AdoptersNeed evidence they can understand and follow
SolanaInfrastructure for an opt-in proof layer
