02 · The gap

Beautiful token. Uploaded PDF underneath.

Most real-world-asset products are strong at the token layer and weak exactly where it matters: the evidence that the physical thing exists and was handled the way somebody claims.

Evidence is an afterthought

Nothing to watch

06 · Economics

Back it early. Own more of the harvest.

A grower opens a plant or batch for backing, adopters stake in, and earlier entry carries a heavier share weight because it carries more risk. Harvest proceeds split pro-rata.

Grower

Documents the grow · keeps the majority share

Adopter

Backs early · weight by stage of entry

Platform

Escrow · market · verification · fee

Seed 3× Growth 2× Flower 1.5× Late 1×

Weights are the designed model on devnet, not a live financial instrument. Nothing here is an offer or a return promise; see the risks page.

07 · Why now

Rules opening. Tooling missing.

European cultivation rules are loosening, which means more people are being asked to document grows properly — and almost none of them have a decent way to do it. Meanwhile RWA capital keeps looking for assets that aren’t another fund wrapper.
