02 · What it is

A journal growers already need.

Growtoo is a free grow journal: plant records, reminders, photos, weather planning, and an AI coach. The asset layer is optional — journal-to-proof infrastructure, not a token that leads.

Records

Plants, feeding, watering, stages

Capture

Photos, reminders, weather planning

Coach

AI that reads the journal, not a blog

Journal first, proof second. Growtoo turns a grower's ongoing plant history into structured evidence that can support more trustworthy, evidence-backed assets.

03 · The problem

Hard to log. Harder to verify.

Grow tracking is often manual, inconsistent, and difficult for someone outside the grower’s process to verify. That makes adoption, collaboration, and any future investment model harder to trust.

Manual tracking

Notes in chats, sheets, and memory

Inconsistent history

Gaps nobody outside the grow can reconstruct

Unverifiable claims

A buyer or backer has to take the grower’s word

04 · Product flow

Log it. Organise it. Prove it only if you opt in.

Growers log plant activity, feeding, watering, growth stages, and photos. Growtoo organises that history into evidence. Selected records can then support optional Solana-based proof — verification-driven tokenisation, not a token for its own sake.

06 · Trust model

History first. Marketplace second.

A plant should become eligible for marketplace treatment only after it has enough documented history. Asset provenance has to be earned in the journal, not declared at mint.

Hypothesis, not a final rule. We are testing a coverage threshold — enough watering or feeding days across the trail, currently around half the days after a two-week window. The 50% lifecycle-logging bar is a hypothesis for testing. The exact rule can change with evidence.

11 · Honest case

A validation round. Not a return story.

The round funds the work needed to test whether reliable grower data can become a trusted foundation for future RWA applications. The main risk is still unproven user retention and the legal structure.

We say

  • Evidence-backed assets and asset provenance
  • Verification-driven tokenisation on Devnet today
  • Harvest-backed assets as a future application, not a live claim

We do not say

  • Guaranteed yield or guaranteed returns
  • Easy ownership of harvest proceeds
  • Investment performance, or that current tokens have monetary value

If the journal does not retain growers, the RWA layer has nothing underneath it. That is the test this raise is for.
