02 · Claims

What we claim, and what we don’t.

This slide comes first on purpose. If a claim you expected is missing from the left column, that’s deliberate.

We do claim

  • The journal is free, works with no wallet, and is shipping today
  • Every mint is a real transaction you can open in a public explorer
  • Adopters see the same trail the grower sees
  • The architecture and the risks are published, not on request

We do not claim

  • That $GROWTOO has value — it’s a devnet test asset
  • Any yield, return, or financial outcome
  • That the system is trustless today
  • That harvest redemption works — it’s specified, not shipped
  • That growtoo is legal advice for your jurisdiction
03 · Trust model

Who you have to trust right now.

The honest answer is: us, at one specific step. A grower could in principle write a journal entry that didn’t happen, so today the platform operator reviews progress before a stage is minted. That is a single point of trust and we name it rather than hide it behind the word “on-chain”.

04 · Where trust moves

Nobody should be trusted forever.

The roadmap is about removing ourselves from the middle of that review step, in the order that actually reduces risk rather than the order that sounds most decentralised.

01

Timestamped evidence

Photos and entries captured in-app, harder to backfill after the fact.

02

Independent attestation

More than one party signing that a stage happened, not just the operator.

03

Redemption with teeth

A real fulfillment path, so a false claim has a consequence at harvest.

Current design and the intended path are written up in the architecture docs.

06 · Risks

The ways this could go wrong.

Listed because they’re real, not because a lawyer asked. Detail and mitigations live on the risks page.

Regulatory

Cultivation and redemption rules differ per country and can move against us.

Fulfillment

Turning a harvest token into a physical counterpart is the hardest unsolved step.

Falsified logs

Self-reported entries can be gamed until attestation is stronger.

Key & wallet loss

Self-custody means a lost key is a lost token. No recovery on our side.

Operator dependence

Today one party reviews mints. That’s a concentration risk until it isn’t.

Migration

Devnet balances aren’t transferable value. Migration rules publish before launch.
