The proof chain

Grow it. Verify it. Own or trade it.

How a journal entry becomes something anyone can check on Solana.

  1. 01

    Grow it.

    Log watering, feeding, and stage changes as they happen. Get reminders and AI-guided next steps so nothing gets missed.

  2. 02

    Verify it.

    Each completed stage — germination, seedling, vegetative, flowering, harvest — gets minted as an on-chain update, linked to your journal entry. Every mint has a real transaction you can check yourself.

  3. 03

    Own or trade it.

    Growers hold Seed RWAs tied to their plants. Adopters can back a grow they trust and follow its verified progress from seed to harvest.

Every stage update links to a real Solana transaction — click through and check it yourself.
