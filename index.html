Journal tokens & seed RWAs Live on devnet

Your grow journal is not just notes — each adopted seed becomes a real-world asset (RWA) on Solana. Mint a Seed NFT, link it to your journal plant, earn $GROW as you grow, trade on the marketplace, and follow every transaction on-chain.

Seed NFT minting

Mint a real Seed NFT for every physical seed you adopt. Strain name, batch code and linked journal plant ID are stored in verifiable metadata on Arweave.

Journal-linked provenance

The NFT and your grow journal stay connected. Document watering, feeding and stage changes in the app — the on-chain record reflects the same plant lifecycle.

Six growth stages on-chain

Advance from Seed → Germination → Seedling → Vegetative → Flowering → Harvest. Each stage updates the NFT metadata with a full growth history log.

Earn $GROW rewards

Collect the $GROW SPL token at every growth milestone — up to 225 $GROW per plant from seed to harvest. Rewards mint directly to your connected wallet.

Real transaction history

Every seed mint and growth step produces a Solana devnet transaction. Inspect signatures in the app and open them on Solscan — full transparency, no simulated hashes.

Multi-wallet connect

Connect Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or enter a watch-only address. Link your wallet to your dnevnik.live account with a signed message for a verified pubkey.

Devnet marketplace

List your minted seed RWAs and flower-stage tokens for $GROW. Browse listings from other growers and buy with a wallet-signed payment — two tradeable asset types.

Redeem or trade harvest

At harvest, your token represents a documented physical plant. Redeem CBD flower from your grow or trade the asset on the marketplace for $GROW and partner products.
