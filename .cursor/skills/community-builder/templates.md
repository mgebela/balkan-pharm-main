# Community templates

Fill these in working files under `community/`. Do not publish from here.

## Daily queue (`community/queue/YYYY-MM-DD.md`)

```markdown
# Queue YYYY-MM-DD

Status: waiting for approval
Reviewed: N public conversations (cap 8)
Selected: N
Skipped: N (one-line reasons)

## 1. [reply | post] — short title

- Platform:
- URL:
- Author (public handle only):
- Topic:
- Why this one: 
- Invite: none | soft | fit
- Claims used: (ids/phrases from claims.md)
- Risk flags: none | legal | medical | financial | person-named | needs-approval
- Status: draft | approved | edit | skip | published

### Draft

[body]

### Notes for human

[anything that needs a personal line, a fact check, or a softer invite]
```

## Original post

Use the same card. Leave URL blank. `Why this one` should name the theme (documentation, coach-without-replacing-judgment, Devnet-without-hype, weekly lesson).

## Soft invite (only when it fits)

One sentence, then the link. Examples:

- “If you want the log, reminders, and coach in one place, growtoo is free to try: https://growto.live/”
- “We’re early and on Solana Devnet — journal works with no wallet.”

Never attach an invite to legal, medical, or investment threads.
