NET

Devnet only: no monetary value

All Seed NFTs and $GROWTOO on growtoo today live on Solana Devnet. They are for product testing. They are not mainnet assets, they are not cash equivalents, and they carry no monetary value.

ASK

How prices form

There is no bonding curve and no price oracle. A grower (or seller) sets an ask in $GROWTOO when they list. An adopter fills that ask through the marketplace escrow. Last price on the board is the most recent listed ask (or sample depth when the live tape is empty).

TRU

Trust model (centralized MVP)

One collection authority currently controls metadata updates, stage reward minting, and escrow settlement. Stage “proof” means the operator checked that a journal advance exists, not an independent oracle, photo attestation, or IoT feed. Fabricated journal entries are a real residual risk for adopters until verification hardens.

RWA

What “adopting” means today

On Devnet, adopting means holding a test RWA and following the linked journal. It does not create a transferable legal title to physical CBD plant material, custody rights, or a guaranteed harvest payout. Redemption into a real harvest share is specified in architecture docs but is still a mock / planned path.

LAW

Regulation & CBD logistics

CBD and related plant material are regulated differently by jurisdiction. Any future physical redemption would need to comply with local law, licensing, custody, shipping, and KYC/AML where required. growtoo does not currently operate a licensed fulfillment pipeline. Nothing on this site is legal, tax, or investment advice.

AUD

Audit & team

The mint-queue authority is a single operational trust point. A third-party audit is planned before any mainnet launch, not claimed as done. Public code and addresses live in the open repository and RWA docs.
